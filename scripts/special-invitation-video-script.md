## Full version (about 4 minutes)

Hi, it's [your name].

I'm recording this for a short list of people. If you're watching, you're one of them.

You might be a speaker, an author, a writer, or someone who facilitates a peer group. You might be a consultant I've worked alongside for years. Or you might be a friend or family member who has been in my corner the whole way. Whichever one you are, this is a special invitation, and I wanted you to hear it from me.

Here's what I know about you. You're already the expert. You've done the work, written the book, given the talk, and helped people get real results.

But being the expert and being *known* as the expert are two different things.

Most of us grow by referral and word of mouth. That's a blessing, but it's unpredictable. When it slows down, we send a burst of messages or post every day for a week, and then life gets busy and it stops.

Think about building a house. You wouldn't hang the front door before you pour the foundation. Authority works the same way. First you build the foundation. Then you invite people in.

So here's my invitation: **start the journey with me and my team.**

It all starts with **The Authority Desk.** About twenty minutes a month on a call with us becomes published articles under your name, written in your voice and edited by real strategists. Over time that becomes a body of work that speaks for you before you ever get on a call.

As part of that process, you'll go through the **Brand Voice Interview.** It's a guided conversation where we capture how you think and how you sound: your tone, your phrases, your point of view, and the stories only you can tell. You do it once, and from then on everything we create sounds unmistakably like you.

And then we bring in **Insta Newsletter.** It takes what you've already said in your calls, articles, and posts and turns it into a branded newsletter that goes out on schedule. It keeps you top of mind with the people who matter.

That's your foundation: your authority, your voice, and a steady way to stay in front of people.

Once that infrastructure is in place, we help you go out and grow. Through **Premium Leads**, we build you a multi-touch outreach strategy around your ideal client: the people you serve best and most want to work with. It isn't one message and done. It's a thoughtful cadence that sets you apart from your competition and grows your network one real relationship at a time. And when those people look you up, they find an authority, because that's exactly what you've built.

A lot of what I believe about this is in my book, ***Marketing Mayhem.*** *(hold up the book)* If you want to understand why the old way of marketing feels so chaotic, and what to do instead, I'd love for you to check it out.

But the most important thing today isn't the book. It's taking the first step.

I'm not sending this to everyone. I'm sending it to people I know, trust, and believe in, because I've seen what you bring to the world, and I want more of the right people to find you.

So here's your next step. Go to **[your link]**, answer a few short questions about you and the clients you want to reach, and let's start the journey together.

If you have questions first, just reply to this message. I'd love to hear from you.

Thank you for watching, and thank you for being someone I wanted to invite. Talk soon.
