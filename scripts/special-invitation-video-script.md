# Premium Leads: A Special Invitation (Recording Script)

**Who it's for:** peers, speakers, authors, writers, peer-group facilitators, consultants, friends and family
**Format:** talking-head video (camera, Zoom or phone)
**Length:** full version is about 4 minutes; a 60-second cut is at the end
**Tone:** warm and personal, like you're talking to one person across the table rather than a room

> **Before you record:** fill in the [brackets]. Words in *italics* are stage directions, so don't read them aloud. A `/` marks a natural pause.

---

## Full version (about 4 minutes)

### 1. The personal opening (0:00–0:30)

*Look straight into the camera. Smile. Slow down.*

Hi, it's [your name]. /

I'm recording this for a short list of people. If you're watching, it's because you're one of them.

You might be a speaker, an author, a writer, or someone who facilitates a peer group. You might be a consultant I've worked beside for years. Or you might be a friend or family member who's been cheering me on. / Whichever you are, this is a **special invitation**, and I wanted you to hear it from me first.

### 2. The problem we share (0:30–1:15)

*Lean in a little. This part should feel like recognition, not a pitch.*

Here's something I've noticed about the people on this list. **You are already the expert.** You've done the work, written the book, given the talk and helped people get real results.

But being the expert and being *known* as the expert are two different things.

Most of us grow our business the same way: referrals, word of mouth, someone who happened to see us speak. That's wonderful, but it's also unpredictable. When things go quiet, it's tempting to send a burst of messages or post a lot for a week, and then life gets busy and it stops.

Think of it like a garden. / You can't plant everything in one weekend and expect a harvest. You plant on purpose, in the right soil, and you tend it a little every week. That's what gets you a harvest season after season.

### 3. The invitation (1:15–1:45)

*Pause before this section. It's the heart of the video.*

So here's my invitation. / **I'd like you to work with my team at Premium Leads.**

Together we'll design an outreach campaign built around *your* ideal client. We'll be clear about who that is, find them, and reach out in a way that sounds like you, so that over time **you're positioned as the authority in your space.**

That's the goal. It's not one campaign and done. It's a system that keeps working for you.

### 4. How it works (1:45–3:15)

*Count the steps on your fingers if that feels natural.*

Let me walk you through it, because there are four pieces and each one builds on the one before.

**First: the Brand Voice Interview.**
Everything starts with a guided conversation, about 45 minutes, led by someone senior on our team. We capture how you think and how you sound: your tone, the phrases you use, your point of view and the stories you tell. You do it once, and it becomes a voice profile that everything else is written from. So nothing we send ever sounds like a template. It sounds like *you*.

**Second: Premium Leads.**
This is where we design your outreach campaign. We define your ideal client profile together, the industries, titles and kinds of people you serve best, and then we reach out to them on purpose, consistently, in your voice. So instead of waiting for referrals, you start real conversations with the people you most want to help.

**Third: The Authority Desk.**
When someone gets a message from you, what do they do next? / They look you up. The Authority Desk makes sure they find a credible expert. About twenty minutes a month on a call with us becomes published articles under your name, written in your voice and edited by real strategists. Over time that becomes a body of work that speaks for you before you ever get on a call.

**And fourth: Insta Newsletter.**
Not everyone is ready to talk the first time they hear from you, and that's okay. Insta Newsletter takes what you've already said in your calls, articles and posts and turns it into a branded newsletter that goes out on schedule. It keeps you top of mind and keeps the relationship warm until the timing is right.

*Beat.*

So you've got **your voice**, **your outreach**, **your authority** and **your follow-through** all working together.

### 5. Why I'm asking you (3:15–3:40)

*Speak from the heart here. Feel free to make this your own.*

I'm not sending this to everyone. I'm sending it to people I know, trust and believe in, because I've seen what you bring to the world, and I want more of the right people to find you.

[Optional: add one sentence about why this matters to you personally, such as a story, a moment or a person who inspired you.]

### 6. The call to action (3:40–4:00)

*Slow down and be specific.*

Here's the next step. / Go to **[your link]** and answer a few short questions about you and the clients you want to reach. It only takes a few minutes. Then we'll find a time to talk and map out your campaign together.

If you have questions first, just reply to this message or reach out to me directly. I'd love to hear from you.

*Smile. Hold a beat before you stop recording.*

Thank you for watching, and thank you for being someone I wanted to invite. / Talk soon.

## 60-second version (for social, text or a short email)

Hi, it's [your name], and this is a **special invitation** for a few people I really value: speakers, authors, writers, peer-group facilitators, consultants, and the friends and family who've been in my corner.

You're already the expert. My team at Premium Leads wants to help more of the right people *know* it.

I'm inviting you to work with us on an outreach campaign built around your ideal client, one that positions you as the authority in your space over time.

We start with a **Brand Voice Interview**, so everything sounds like you. **Premium Leads** reaches your ideal clients consistently. **The Authority Desk** publishes articles under your name, so you're credible when people look you up. And **Insta Newsletter** keeps every relationship warm.

If that sounds good, go to **[your link]**, answer a few quick questions, and let's talk.

Thanks for being someone I wanted to invite.

## Recording tips

- **Talk to one person.** Picture a specific friend or colleague on the list and talk to them.
- **Put the camera at eye level** with a window or soft light in front of you, not behind you.
- **Paraphrase freely.** The script is a map, not a cage, and your natural words will land better than reading them perfectly.
- **Record the 60-second version separately** rather than cutting it from the long one. It'll feel tighter.
- **Pin the link** in the caption or email body, since people won't remember a URL they only heard.
